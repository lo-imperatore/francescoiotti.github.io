I am a PhD student in the **National PhD Program in Robotics and Intelligent Machines (PhD-RIM)** at the Department of Information Engineering of the **University of Pisa**, supervised by Prof. Manolo Garabini and Prof. Franco Angelini. My thesis is titled *Planning and Control Techniques for High-Level Autonomy of Robots*.

My research focuses on the mechatronic design, integration, motion planning and control of modular mobile robots, with particular emphasis on legged and hybrid locomotion. My experience spans mechanical interfaces, series elastic actuators, kinematic control, dynamics identification and ROS/ROS 2 hardware–software integration. I develop and experimentally evaluate control and state estimation methods for robotic platforms, including reinforcement learning for locomotion and global planning with multiple motion models.

Research interests
------
- Legged, wheeled-legged and tracked-legged (hybrid) locomotion
- Proprioceptive state estimation for legged robots
- Motion planning with multiple motion models
- Series elastic actuators and dynamics identification
- ROS 2 hardware interfaces for modular mobile robots
